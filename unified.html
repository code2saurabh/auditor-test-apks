Analytics Auditor

Say what the app is supposed to send. Give it a recording of what the app actually sent. It tells you the difference — and who else received the data along the way.

RUN

Audit an app

Upload an app · it runs itself
Drop an .apk or .apkm here or click to choose one · any size

One per line. tap TARGET -> event_name ties an event to the tap that should cause it; screen NAME and consent SIGNAL granted are run-level. Anything the app fires that you did not list is reported as unexpected — which is how a wrong name gets caught, because the right one shows as missing beside it.

iOS journeys run on a real device through BrowserStack, driven by the Appium tapper, so the routes above are automated end to end. The upload must be an .ipa.

Choose an APK to begin.

Kept in this browser only. The BrowserStack key never leaves the runner — it is read from that machine's environment and is never accepted from this page.

The HAR shows who received data. The phone log shows what Firebase actually recorded — the item, its variant, the price, the quantity — because the Android SDK hands its events to Google Play Services, which is a different process from the app and is not in the HAR at all.
